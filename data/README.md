# Data boundary

This directory is reserved for **reuse-safe derived data** that match the final published article and Supplementary Materials.

Do not upload raw third-party exports or coordinate archives when redistribution is restricted by source or platform terms. Instead, document the source, access date, unit/role, and retrieval route.

The published study reports 7,410 admitted candidate/amenity records and 5,097 inside-boundary candidates. These values must not be altered in this archive unless the authors and publisher issue a documented correction.

The repository should remain a public-data screening and robustness-audit archive; it does not provide demand validation, feeder-capacity validation, financial feasibility, power-flow validation, or construction approval.
