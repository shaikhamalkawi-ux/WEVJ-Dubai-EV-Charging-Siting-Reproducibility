# Attribution and reuse notes

## Dubai government open-data layers

Unless a dataset-specific licence states otherwise, admitted Dubai Pulse/Data Dubai open-data layers in this extension are treated under the Dubai Open Data Licence published by Dubai Pulse.

Licence: https://www.dubaipulse.gov.ae/docs/DDE%20_%20DRAFT_Open_Data%20Licence_LONG_Form_English%203.pdf

Policy basis: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2017/Resolution%20No.%20%282%29%20of%202017.html

The licence grants a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive right to use the information for commercial and non-commercial purposes, subject to its conditions. For work products using the information, retain supplied creator/originator identification, copyright notices, and a URL/hyperlink to the original material where reasonably practicable. Do not imply endorsement by the Dubai Government or source entity. Adapted material must follow the licence's downstream-sharing conditions.

### Proposed attribution format

> Source data: [Issuing Authority], [Dataset Title], Data Dubai / Dubai Pulse, accessed [date], [official dataset URL]. Reused under the Dubai Open Data Licence. The authors' processing and interpretation are independent and do not imply endorsement by the Government of Dubai or the issuing authority.

## Parking layer

The UAE Open Data mirror currently displays `Number of Parking Spaces Per Zone` as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Before public upload, the exact Dubai resource URL and retained file hash must also be locked.

## OpenStreetMap / Geofabrik

If any OSM-derived file is redistributed, comply with ODbL 1.0 and OpenStreetMap attribution requirements. For this project the preferred treatment is LINK-ONLY for the large historical Geofabrik PBF, with source URL, access date, snapshot timestamp, and SHA-256 recorded.

## Google-derived material

Raw Google Maps Platform / Places / Geocoding outputs are excluded from redistribution. Only reuse-safe derived statements already admitted in the published paper/package may be retained.
